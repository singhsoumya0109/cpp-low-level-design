## Primary Actors
- **User / Client**: Primary interacting entity.
- **Admin / Operator**: Privileged operations and maintenance.
- **System**: Background worker or scheduled monitors.

## Use Case Scenarios
1. **Scenario 1**: Standard successful execution flow.
2. **Scenario 2**: Validation failure or boundary edge case.
3. **Scenario 3**: High contention or concurrent execution flow.
